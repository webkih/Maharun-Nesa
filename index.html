About / Profile

Maharun Nesa Mukti is a purpose-driven youth leader committed to combining academic rigor with meaningful social change. She completed her Honors in Botany from the University of Dhaka, where she strengthened a scientific mindset grounded in discipline, inquiry, and responsibility.

Across her college and university life, she served as an active member of the Bangladesh National Cadet Corps, developing resilience, leadership under pressure, and a deep respect for teamwork and service.

As Vice President of the Leo Club of Eden College under Lions International youth initiatives, Maharun has become a trusted voice for young people, community development, and public engagement. Her work reflects integrity, consistency, and a global mindset suited for scholarships, partnerships, and international programs.
